02 / ABOUT THE CREATIVE
OTTAWA / CANADA

A designer with a point of view

Making digital work feel human.

Alvin Tumusiime is an emerging interactive media designer and digital creative from Ottawa, Ontario.

He combines web design, visual branding, and social-media strategy to create engaging digital experiences.

As an Interactive Media Design student at Algonquin College, he continues to develop practical skills in HTML, CSS, Figma, and digital content creation.

His work helps artists, entrepreneurs, and event promoters present their ideas with clarity and style.

Alvin’s creative strengths include:

  • 01 Designing responsive websites
  • 02 Creating promotional graphics and social-media content
  • 03 Developing memorable brands for events and emerging businesses

He approaches every project with curiosity, collaboration, and attention to detail.

Alvin is especially interested in creating digital tools that connect audiences with music, entertainment, and local events.

His goal is to use design and technology to help people, brands, and communities stand out online.

Featured case study / Group 8 — EVO

Drake: Entrepreneurial Mindset.

TORONTO / OVO
OVO

Innovation, lyrics, and identity

This presentation studies Aubrey “Drake” Graham’s development from actor and rapper into a major entrepreneur and cultural brand builder. The project explores how creativity, consistency, strategic thinking, and identity helped shape his long-term impact.

2001

Early career

Drake began his public career as an actor on the Canadian television series Degrassi.

2006

Starting with little

His early music journey included small returns, including only $304.04 in royalties from Room for Improvement.

2007

Building OVO

October’s Very Own became a foundation for developing an independent identity, label, community, and lifestyle brand.

2009

Expanding reach

So Far Gone helped grow his audience and led to a major industry opportunity with Young Money.

Key skills

Leadership Creativity Branding Risk-taking Networking Strategic thinking

Personal traits

Perseverance Adaptability Confidence Ambition Vision Determination

Success formula

Consistency + creativity + strategic diversification.

Drake’s story shows how creators can build systems, own their narrative, and treat their audience as a community rather than only as customers.

Project conclusion

Talent creates attention.
Strategy creates longevity.

The central lesson of this project is that success is not overnight luck. Setbacks can become fuel for independence, while a strong brand can grow beyond one industry through thoughtful diversification.
